# SOURCE REGISTRY — AI / OPENAI / AGENT SIGNALS

## Source scoring
Score each source on:
- Directness: firsthand / reporting / commentary
- Evidence: documents/data vs opinion
- Relevance to active $10M jobs
- Freshness
- Independence from other sources

Confidence labels:
- HIGH — firsthand/documented + corroborated.
- MEDIUM — credible but incomplete/contested.
- LOW — prediction/opinion requiring testing.

## Watch sources
1. The Diary Of A CEO — AI/OpenAI interviews: hypothesis generator.
2. Official frontier-model provider announcements/docs: product capability confirmation.
3. Independent benchmarks/evals: capability evidence.
4. Customer interviews and our telemetry: highest priority for product/business decisions.
5. Regulatory/governance authorities: enterprise constraints.
6. Competitor/product pricing pages: distribution/offer intelligence.

## Rule
External media may create a JOB. It cannot mark a JOB as DONE.
