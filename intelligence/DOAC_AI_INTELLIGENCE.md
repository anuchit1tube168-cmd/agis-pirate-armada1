# DOAC AI INTELLIGENCE MAP — PROJECT $10M

Last updated: 2026-09-18

## Purpose
Use The Diary Of A CEO AI interviews as a recurring external-signal source, not as unquestioned truth.

Every insight must be stored as:

```
SOURCE → CLAIM → CONFIDENCE → BUSINESS IMPLICATION → TESTABLE EXPERIMENT → METRIC → DECISION
```

No forecast becomes a product decision without an experiment or corroborating evidence.

---

## Tier A — Direct OpenAI / OpenAI-insider relevance

### Daniel Kokotajlo — former OpenAI researcher
Episode: “ChatGPT Whistleblower: They Offered Me $2m To Keep Quiet, No One Is Ready For What's Coming!” (2026-07-13)

Signals:
- AI capability growth may accelerate through automation of cognitive work and AI R&D.
- White-collar work could shift toward directing/working with AI systems.
- AI safety/alignment, governance, incentives and control remain core uncertainties.
- Forecast timelines and catastrophe probabilities are Kokotajlo's judgments, not established facts.

$10M implication:
1. Build an AI workforce control layer, not a single model wrapper.
2. Make model providers replaceable.
3. Measure human-hours displaced/augmented per workflow.
4. Build permissions, audit, evaluation and approval into the core.
5. Automate our own build/test/research loop first.

Project mapping:
- Workflow
- Evaluation
- Permissions
- Audit
- Data

Experiments:
- Requirement → Plan → Build → Test → Review → Staging pipeline.
- Compare founder-hours before/after skill/template extraction.
- Measure safe automation rate and intervention rate.

---

### Karen Hao — investigative journalist focused on OpenAI
Episode: “AI Whistleblower: We Are Being Gaslit By AI Companies, They're Hiding The Truth!” (2026-03-26)

Signals:
- Concentration of power and incentives inside frontier AI companies should be treated as a governance risk.
- Claims around AGI, safety and competitive urgency should be separated from evidence.
- Organizational incentives can conflict with stated missions.
- Human/resource/environmental costs belong in total-cost analysis.

$10M implication:
1. Avoid dependence on one frontier vendor.
2. Keep source provenance for strategic claims.
3. Create an explicit governance/economic layer.
4. Track total cost of an AI outcome rather than token price alone.
5. Never let marketing claims substitute for evaluation.

Project mapping:
- Integration
- Evaluation
- Audit
- Permissions
- Cost/Math

Experiments:
- Multi-model router benchmark.
- Source-confidence labels in decision records.
- Cost-per-success dashboard.

## Tier B — Technical / governance cross-checks

### Geoffrey Hinton
Episode: “Godfather of AI: They Keep Silencing Me But I'm Trying to Warn Them!” (2025-06-16)

Signals:
- Rapid capability gains can create cyber, labor and control risks.
- AI may produce large productivity benefits alongside large downside risks.
- Capability and safety should be evaluated separately.

$10M implication:
- Security/Eval Agent must independently test autonomy and tool access.
- Productivity gains are only counted when quality remains above threshold.
- Security becomes product value, not a compliance appendix.

---

### Yoshua Bengio
Episode: “Godfather of AI: We Have 2 Years Before Everything Changes!” (2025-12-18)

Signals:
- Agentic systems introduce risks different from passive chat systems.
- Goal-directed behavior and tool use increase the need for control mechanisms.
- Regulation/governance lag can create enterprise hesitation.

$10M implication:
- Human approval gates, sandboxing and least privilege are commercial differentiators.
- ASTRA should sell “controlled autonomy,” not “full autonomy.”

---

### Tristan Harris
Episode: “AI Expert: Here Is What The World Looks Like In 2 Years!” (2025-11-27)

Signals:
- Competitive incentives can cause organizations to deploy faster than governance matures.
- AI workers can increase efficiency of AI development itself.
- Social/economic transition planning matters if labor displacement accelerates.

$10M implication:
- Our internal agent factory should create a speed loop.
- Customer-facing messaging should focus on transition, oversight and measured ROI.
- Do not reward unsafe speed.

---

### Mo Gawdat
Episode: “Ex-Google Officer: You Only Have 3 Years Left Before It Hits!” (2026-06-01)

Signals:
- Strong claims about job displacement and AGI timelines are forecasts, not facts.
- He emphasizes large productivity leverage for users who learn to work with AI.
- Economic models may be stressed if labor substitution outpaces adaptation.

$10M implication:
- Train customers to manage AI workers, not only buy software.
- Add “human supervisor productivity” as a KPI.
- Build training/enablement as a distribution and retention channel.

---

### Mustafa Suleyman
Episode (2023): interview on containment and “The Coming Wave”

Signals:
- Powerful general-purpose technologies create a containment/control problem.
- AI capability diffusion means control cannot depend only on model creators.
- Governance must be operational, not only policy.

$10M implication:
- ASTRA control plane should remain model-agnostic.
- Governance-as-code becomes a durable product layer.

## Convergence map

## What we DO NOT conclude
- We do not assume AGI arrives on any specific date.
- We do not assume any guest's probability forecast is correct.
- We do not assume job-loss percentages are established facts.
- We do not build based on fear.
- We use the channel as an early-warning / hypothesis source.

## Decision rule
When 2+ independent high-quality sources converge on a signal AND the signal can materially affect Revenue, Distribution, Reuse, Speed, Margin, Risk, Customer ROI or Learning Velocity:
1. create a testable project hypothesis;
2. assign an agent;
3. run a bounded experiment;
4. measure;
5. promote to strategy only after evidence.
